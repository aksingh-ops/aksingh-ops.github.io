About Me

Technical depth.
Business fluency.

Most analytics teams are good at one of two things: building technically rigorous models, or communicating findings that leadership actually acts on. I do both. That is the gap I have spent three years learning to close.

At the Iowa Department of Management, I migrated data, reports, and dashboards from a Tyler Technologies legacy system to GCP BigQuery, handling source-to-target mapping, ETL execution, JSON/REST API integrations, and validation across seven state departments. That project taught me that the hardest part of data work is rarely the analysis. It is knowing what to validate, who to ask, and how to explain what you found.

Every project in this portfolio starts with a written business question and ends with a recommendation in plain English. That is by design, not accident.
